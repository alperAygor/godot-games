# Privacy Policy — Clothesline Sort

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]
**Developer / data controller:** [DEVELOPER NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS], Türkiye
**Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL]
**Policy URL:** [POLICY URL]

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play "Clothesline Sort" (also known in Turkish as "Çamaşır İpi"), a free puzzle game for Android ("the Game").

## 1. The short version

- The Game has **no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no user-generated content.**
- We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and the Game does not request those permissions.
- Your progress (levels, coins, settings, purchases status) is stored **only on your device**. We do not run a server that receives it.
- The Game shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Google's ad software collects some device information to show and measure ads (see section 3).
- You can remove interstitial ads with a **one-time purchase** processed by **Google Play**.
- The Game needs an **internet connection** to run.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves, locally on your device: your level progress, coins, unlocked looks and boosters, daily-task progress, sound and language settings, and whether you purchased "remove ads". This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the Game's storage or uninstall the Game. We cannot access it, and we cannot recover it for you. (If Android's device backup is enabled for the Game, your device backup service may copy it under Google's own terms.)

## 3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example, to get one extra rack spot or to double coins after a win). To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service running, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, according to Google's published guidance for the SDK:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of a device);
- user interactions with the Game and ads (for example, launching the Game, taps and video views);
- diagnostic information (for example, start-up time, hang rate, energy use);
- device and account identifiers, including the **Android advertising ID** and the app set ID.

Sources: Google's data safety guidance for the Google Mobile Ads SDK — https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure . All data the SDK collects is encrypted in transit using TLS, per that guidance.

**Your choices.**
- *Android settings:* you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Settings (Google > Ads). If you delete the ID, apps receive a string of zeros instead.
- *In the Game:* where required by law (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform the first time you play, and a **"Privacy options"** button in the Game's info/settings page lets you review or change your choice. Without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads may be shown.
- *United States state laws:* where applicable, you can use the same privacy options to limit use of your data for personalized advertising.

Google acts as an independent controller for its advertising purposes. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy ) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ).

## 4. Purchases (Google Play)

The "remove ads" purchase is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Google Play**. Payment details are handled by Google and **never reach us**. The Game receives from Google Play only the information needed to know whether you own the product (for example, product ID and purchase state) and to restore it if you reinstall. We do not receive your name, card or address. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Google Play.

## 5. Internet connection check

Because the Game requires an internet connection, it sends a plain HTTPS request (no cookies and no identifiers added by us) to connectivity-check addresses operated by Google (`connectivitycheck.gstatic.com`) and Cloudflare (`cp.cloudflare.com`) to see whether you are online. These operators necessarily see your IP address as part of any internet request. We do not send them any personal information from the Game.

## 6. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or sell your personal information, and we do not have accounts, a backend, analytics services (at the time of this policy), location, contacts, or any user-generated content. We do not sell personal information.

## 7. Children

The Game is **not designed for or directed at children under 13**, and Google Play's target audience for the Game is set accordingly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that involves personal information, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 9. International transfers

Google and Cloudflare operate globally; your data may be processed in countries other than your own, under their own safeguards.

## 10. Retention and deletion

The Game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Local data is removed by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it. Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's retention practices; you can delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

## 11. Security

Data the Game sends to Google is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by your device's security.
